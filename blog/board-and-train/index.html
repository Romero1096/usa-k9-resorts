Is Board & Train Right for Your Dog?

August 28, 2026USA K9 Resorts

Board & Train is the program we recommend most often for owners who want fast, lasting results. Your dog stays with us at our Odessa facility and receives three structured training sessions every single day. In one week, we can cover more ground than months of once-a-week lessons.

What does a typical day look like? Morning session focuses on foundational obedience — sit, down, stay, come, heel. Afternoon is more dynamic: leash work, boundary training, distraction proofing. Evening is often socialization and confidence building, especially for dogs that are anxious or reactive.

Board & Train works best for dogs that are motivated, adaptable, and don't have severe separation anxiety. It's also ideal for busy owners who want results but can't commit to daily training sessions at home. We've worked with dogs of all breeds and ages — from 8-week-old puppies to 8-year-old dogs with deeply ingrained habits.

One thing we always emphasize: the training doesn't end when your dog comes home. We do a thorough handoff session with every owner, walking you through exactly what your dog learned and how to maintain it. Consistency on your end is what makes the results stick.

Programs start at $1,225/week or $200/day with a 3-night minimum. We have only 20 spaces at our facility, so availability is limited. Call or text 832-381-8150 to check availability and schedule your dog's evaluation.
