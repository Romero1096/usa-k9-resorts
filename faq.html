Private or group training sessions

We operate a professional dog training facility in Odessa, TX. Most of our K9 guests are in different stages of the training and education process. We offer three formats to fit your schedule and goals:

BOARD & TRAIN — Your dog stays with us and receives 3 training sessions per day. This is the fastest way to accelerate your dog's training program. Programs start at $1,225/week or $200/day and include obedience, leash etiquette, boundary training, off-leash work, public access, socialization, and confidence building.

IN HOME LESSONS — A great option if you want to be directly involved in the training process or if you have multiple dogs. Our trainer comes to your home for a personalized session. Initial evaluation is $200.00 for a 2-hour session.

GROUP LESSONS — Offered at our Odessa facility. An excellent introduction to different training methods and a great way to socialize your dog in a structured environment. $50.00 per one-hour session.

Overnight boarding rates

WHAT'S INCLUDED — Your dog will receive attentive care, exercise, feeding per your instructions, and a safe, comfortable environment. Dogs in our care are treated like family.
